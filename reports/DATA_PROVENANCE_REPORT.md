## 3. Provenance Disclaimers & Limitations
1. **Synthetic / Benchmark Dataset (`SRC_TN_BENCHMARK_2024`):** The 450 statewide baseline observations were generated using empirical pedotransfer functions parameterized on peninsular Indian soil baselines. Until independent ground truth surveys are conducted across all 38 districts, these values are designated as a calibrated scientific demonstration.
2. **Analytical Protocols:** Official soil pollution enforcement must adhere strictly to EPA Method 3050B (acid digestion) followed by certified ICP-MS/OES laboratory instrumentation with multi-element calibration standards.
