Scientific Distinction: Agronomic Soil Fertility vs Toxic Industrial Contamination

A soil can possess high fertility (adequate N, P, K, and neutral pH) while simultaneously carrying severe industrial toxic heavy-metal contamination (e.g., Hexavalent Chromium in Ranipet SIPCOT or Lead in Manali). Soil-health parameters are tracked independently from heavy-metal indices to prevent conflation of agricultural productivity with environmental toxicity.
