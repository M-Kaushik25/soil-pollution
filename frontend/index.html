Multi-Criteria Spatial Acquisition Function

Candidate monitoring locations across Tamil Nadu are ranked using a normalized composite objective:

$$\text{Acquisition Score}(s) = 0.40 \cdot \text{Risk}(s) + 0.40 \cdot \text{Uncertainty}(s) + 0.20 \cdot \text{Spatial Dispersion}(s)$$
Risk Criterion ($40\%$): Prioritizes hotspots exceeding ecological risk thresholds ($RI$).
Uncertainty Criterion ($40\%$): Prioritizes points where the model exhibits high epistemic variance.
Spatial Dispersion ($20\%$): Minimum Euclidean distance ($\text{MinDist}$) to existing samples to prevent spatial clustering.

Scientific Disclosure

Data Provenance & Research Characterization

Dataset Provenance: The benchmark dataset ($N=446$, depth $0\text{–}15\text{ cm}$) models topsoil geochemical distributions across Tamil Nadu industrial and agricultural transects (Coimbatore-Tiruppur, Ranipet, Salem, Cauvery Delta, Chennai-Ennore). Samples were calibrated using published geochemical literature and EPA 3050B digestion conventions.

Environmental Covariate Formulas: The 35 environmental covariates are derived from regional parametric elevation gradients (SRTM proxy), lapse-rate thermal gradients (WorldClim proxy), and Euclidean proximity to industrial centers. In an operational production deployment, these would be retrieved from live Google Earth Engine rasters.

Spatial Cross-Validation Reality: Standard Random 5-Fold CV yields $R^2$ values of $0.18\text{–}0.43$. However, rigorous Spatial Block 5-Fold Cross Validation results in negative $R^2$ values across out-of-fold spatial blocks. This highlights the fundamental challenge of spatial autocorrelation and out-of-domain extrapolation in regional digital soil mapping, which this platform documents transparently rather than concealing.
